About Daily Design

Daily Design is a public archive of AI design research digests from Great Creation Studios: four editions a day on how AI is changing creative work, across image, video, 3D, code/UI, sound, games and mashups.

Daily Design since Oct 16, 2026

Four editions a day

  • 7 AM PDT AI Design DailyThe briefing
  • Noon PDT AI Design in UseThe field report
  • 6 PM PDT Best of the DayThe best-of
  • 8 PM PDT Hottest Right NowThe hot list

Editions are dated and timed in Pacific time (Vancouver), shown as PDT. If your clock differs, your own local time is shown in brackets.

The archive and sub access

Starting Nov 8, 2026, editions dated Nov 1, 2026 and later will become sub access (members only) 7 days after they publish. Everything dated through Oct 31, 2026 stays free forever.

How it works

Every edition links straight to the original creators and sources. Engagement numbers were read when each edition was compiled and will have changed since. Sponsors never affect what we pick or how it ranks.

The whole archive is searchable: search every edition and link, then filter by medium, type, edition and date, or browse the archive calendar day by day.

Questions, tips, corrections or sponsorship: see Contact.

Every creator, studio and lab we feature goes on our public X list, Daily Design Creators ↗, with new ones added after every edition.

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookie-free: no cookies, no personal data and no cross-site tracking.
